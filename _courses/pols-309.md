# POLS 309: Race, Culture and Public Policy

While taking this course, I studied the history of public policies in the United States that targeted Native Americans, African Americans, Asian immigrants and Asian Americans, and Latino immigrants and Latino Americans. This gave me a deeper understanding of the history of the United States and immigration, as well as how that history has shaped the current landscape of American public policy. During the course, I completed film analyses of four different documentaries; three are included below. For my analyses, I chose to focus on Latino immigration across all documentaries to deepen my understanding of how past policies have shaped current attitudes toward immigration from South America to the United States.

This course is currently in progress, and will be completed in May 2026.
